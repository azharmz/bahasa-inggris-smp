## Phase D Position

Fase D is the phase relevant to SMP/MTs/Program Paket B. The 2025 guide states that English learning in Phase D focuses on oral, written, and multimodal English in simple texts and expects an end-of-phase level approximately equivalent to CEFR A2.

The guide also gives a vocabulary orientation of approximately **2,000 high-frequency words** for Phase D/A2. These words should be learned in text/context, not as isolated vocabulary lists.

**Important interpretation:** A2 is an end-of-Phase-D orientation, not a claim that a Grade VIII student currently has A2 ability and not a per-semester mastery threshold.

## Official CP — Fase D

### 1. Menyimak–Berbicara (Listening–Speaking)

At the end of Phase D, students are expected to understand the overall flow of information, main ideas, and detailed information from oral texts on everyday topics or topics of interest. They use English to express ideas and experiences orally in various text types about discussed topics, using simple and compound sentences in formal and informal contexts as appropriate.

### 2. Membaca–Memirsa (Reading–Viewing)

At the end of Phase D, students are expected to understand the flow of information and explicit and implicit information from various written or multimodal text types on everyday topics or topics of interest, and respond appropriately to context.

### 3. Menulis–Mempresentasikan (Writing–Presenting)

At the end of Phase D, students communicate ideas and experiences through various written or multimodal text types on everyday topics or topics of interest, beginning to use simple and compound sentences with appropriate text structures and language features. They also express opinions and defend arguments on everyday issues/topics of interest.

## Design Consequences for This Project

The CP is a **phase-end destination**, not a weekly syllabus. Therefore this repo must derive a Grade VIII pathway without pretending that all Phase D outcomes must already be mastered at the current starting point.

The CP also makes clear that the curriculum cannot be reduced to grammar sequencing. The operational map must preserve three integrated element groups:

1. Listening–Speaking;
2. Reading–Viewing;
3. Writing–Presenting.

Grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and text structure are enabling resources for those competencies.

## Relationship to the 2022 Textbook

The Grade VIII textbook predates the 2025 guide but is structurally compatible in important ways: it integrates listening, speaking, reading, viewing, writing, and representing/presenting and uses text-based/genre-based learning. However, compatibility must be mapped rather than assumed. The textbook audit remains a separate evidence layer.
