## What the Book Says About Its Role

The book's foreword states that the textbook may serve as a reference/inspiration and may be modified in designing learning according to student characteristics, potential, and needs. This supports the project's rule that textbook sequence is not automatically the classroom pacing authority.

## Stated Approach

The preface describes a Genre-Based Approach combined with language-acquisition progression from oral toward written language. Skills are integrated across:

- listening;
- speaking;
- reading;
- viewing;
- writing;
- representing.

The preface identifies the main text types for Grade VIII as:

- recount — introduced in Grade VIII;
- narrative — introduced in Grade VIII;
- descriptive — previously taught in Grade VII, reused with new topics;
- procedure — previously taught in Grade VII, reused with new topics.

## Book Structure

### Chapter 0 — The Beginning
Support/review material for later chapters. The book's usage guide lists, among others: classroom language, complimentary/exclamatory expressions, certainty/uncertainty, measurement, surprise/disbelief, agreement, question tags, wishes, adjectives, sympathy, degree verbs, transition signals, disappointment, comparative/superlative, phrasal verbs, and attracting attention.

**Audit note:** Chapter 0 is broad support material. It must not be assumed that every item is a prerequisite that must be taught sequentially before Chapter 1.

### Chapter 1 — Celebrating Independence Day

#### Unit 1 — The Champion of Panjat Pinang
- Function: talk about personal experiences in the past.
- Language features: past tense; regular and irregular past verbs.
- Listening/viewing: conversations about Independence Day experiences.
- Speaking: ask and give information about those experiences.

#### Unit 2 — Going to a Parade
- Function: identify specific information about personal experiences.
- Language features: past tense; time connectives.
- Listening/viewing: conversations about Independence Day experiences.
- Reading: recount texts for specific information.

The table of contents names Unit 3 as *Independence Day at SMP Merdeka*. ## Preliminary Dependency Observations — NOT YET FROZEN

The scope-and-sequence alone suggests recurring dependencies around:

- understanding subject/predicate and basic sentence meaning;
- basic verb recognition/use;
- past-time reference and past verb forms;
- question formation;
- basic vocabulary for familiar contexts;
- sequencing/connectives;
- extracting specific information from short oral/written texts;
- moving from model → guided production → independent production.

These are **preliminary audit observations**, not yet a prerequisite map. They must be checked against actual chapter/unit activities and official CP before freezing.
