# Teaching Package — Subject Pronouns + To Be

- **Kelas:** VIII SMP
- **Semester:** Ganjil / Semester 1
- **Status:** Materi dasar tercatat sudah diajarkan dalam teaching log; latihan dalam file ini adalah dokumentasi/paket rujukan dan tidak otomatis berarti semua latihan telah dilaksanakan.
- **Classification:** FOUNDATION
- **Durasi rancangan:** 1 JP (40 menit)

## Tujuan
Siswa dapat mengenali subject pronouns dan memilih am/is/are dalam kalimat sederhana.

## 1. Subject pronouns
| Pronoun | Arti ringkas |
|---|---|
| I | saya |
| You | kamu/kalian |
| He | dia laki-laki |
| She | dia perempuan |
| It | benda/hewan |
| We | kami/kita |
| They | mereka |

## 2. To be
| Subject | To be | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am a student. |
| You | are | You are kind. |
| He | is | He is tired. |
| She | is | She is happy. |
| It | is | It is small. |
| We | are | We are ready. |
| They | are | They are students. |

Dalam pola ini, to be diikuti kata benda atau kata sifat. Action verbs seperti eat/read/play memerlukan pola berbeda dan tidak dicampur dalam latihan awal ini.

## 3. Positive, negative, question
- She is happy.
- She is not happy.
- Is she happy? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

- They are students.
- They are not students.
- Are they students? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

## Latihan A — Choose am/is/are
1. I ___ happy.
2. He ___ a student.
3. We ___ ready.
4. It ___ small.
5. They ___ tired.

## Latihan B — Make it negative
1. She is late.
2. They are happy.
3. I am tired.

## Latihan C — Make a question
1. He is a teacher.
2. You are ready.
3. She is hungry.

## Kunci jawaban
A: 1 am, 2 is, 3 are, 4 is, 5 are.  
B: 1 She is not late. 2 They are not happy. 3 I am not tired.  
C: 1 Is he a teacher? 2 Are you ready? 3 Is she hungry?

## Alur 40 menit
- Retrieval pronouns: 5 menit
- Model tabel pronoun + be: 10 menit
- Latihan pilihan dan transformasi: 12 menit
- Pair oral practice: 9 menit
- Exit check: 4 menit

## Evidence
Periksa pilihan am/is/are serta kemampuan mengubah bentuk positif, negatif, dan tanya. Teaching log mencatat latihan lisan formatif pernah dilakukan; baseline formal tetap memerlukan diagnostic terpisah.
