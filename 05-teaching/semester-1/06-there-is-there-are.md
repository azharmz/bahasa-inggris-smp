# Teaching Package — There is / There are

- **Kelas:** VIII SMP
- **Semester:** Ganjil / Semester 1
- **Status:** Materi tercatat sudah diajarkan (konfirmasi guru).
- **Classification:** FOUNDATION
- **Durasi rancangan:** 1 JP (40 menit)

## Tujuan
Siswa dapat memilih there is untuk satu benda dan there are untuk lebih dari satu benda.

## Pola inti
- **There is + singular noun**
- **There are + plural noun**

Contoh:
- There is a book.
- There is a pen on the table.
- There are two books.
- There are three pens in the bag.

## Singular dan plural
- one book → a book
- two books
- one pen → a pen
- three pens

Fokus awal pada jumlah benda yang dapat dilihat langsung di kelas. Jangan menambah banyak aturan plural sekaligus.

## Latihan A — Choose
1. There (is/are) a book on the desk.
2. There (is/are) three pens.
3. There (is/are) a bag under the chair.
4. There (is/are) two students near the door.

## Latihan B — Look and say
Gunakan benda nyata di kelas. Siswa mengucapkan satu kalimat untuk satu benda dan satu kalimat untuk beberapa benda.

## Latihan C — Complete
1. There ___ a pencil in my bag.
2. There ___ four chairs in the room.
3. There ___ an English book on the table.

## Kunci jawaban
A: 1 is, 2 are, 3 is, 4 are.  
C: 1 is, 2 are, 3 is.

- Demonstrasi benda nyata: 8 menit
- Model pola: 6 menit
- Latihan pilihan dan completion: 10 menit
- Pair description: 6 menit
- Exit check: 4 menit
