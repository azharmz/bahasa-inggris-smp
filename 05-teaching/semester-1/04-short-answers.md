# Teaching Package — Short Answers

- **Kelas:** VIII SMP
- **Semester:** Ganjil / Semester 1
- **Status:** Pola short answers tercatat sudah diajarkan; file ini menjadi rujukan materi dan latihan.
- **Classification:** FOUNDATION
- **Durasi rancangan:** 1 JP (40 menit)

## Tujuan
Siswa dapat menjawab pertanyaan yes/no dengan short answers yang sesuai dengan subject dan to be.

## Model
| Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| Are you tired? | Yes, I am. | No, I'm not. |
| Is he late? | Yes, he is. | No, he isn't. |
| Is she hungry? | Yes, she is. | No, she isn't. |
| Are they students? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |

## Ingat
- Pertanyaan dengan **Is he ...?** dijawab **Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.**
- Pertanyaan dengan **Are they ...?** dijawab **Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.**
- Jangan menjawab hanya “Yes, he” atau “No, they”.

## Latihan A — Choose
1. Is she tired? (Yes) → __________
2. Are they ready? (No) → __________
3. Is he a student? (Yes) → __________
4. Are you hungry? (No) → __________

## Latihan B — Pair practice
A membacakan pertanyaan; B menjawab dengan kartu Yes/No dan short answer yang sesuai. Tukar peran.

## Kunci jawaban
1. Yes, she is.
2. No, they aren't.
3. Yes, he is.
4. No, I'm not.

## Alur 40 menit
- Retrieval to be: 6 menit
- Model short answers: 8 menit
- Latihan tertulis: 8 menit
- Pair practice: 14 menit
- Exit check: 4 menit

## Evidence
Catat apakah siswa memilih auxiliary/to be yang tepat, bukan hanya apakah jawabannya cocok dengan kartu Yes/No.
