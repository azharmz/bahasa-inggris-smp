# Teaching Package — Possessive Adjectives

- **Kelas:** VIII SMP
- **Semester:** Ganjil / Semester 1
- **Status:** Set awal my/your/his/her tercatat sudah diajarkan.
- **Classification:** FOUNDATION
- **Durasi rancangan:** 1 JP (40 menit)

## Tujuan
Siswa dapat menggunakan my, your, his, dan her sebelum kata benda dalam frasa sederhana.

## Kata kunci
| Possessive adjective | Arti ringkas | Example |
|---|---|---|
| my | milik saya | my book |
| your | milikmu/kalian | your bag |
| his | milik dia laki-laki | his pen |
| her | milik dia perempuan | her pencil |

Possessive adjective ditempatkan sebelum noun: **my book**, bukan **book my**.

## Contoh kalimat
- This is my book.
- Your pencil is red.
- His bag is black.
- Her mother is a teacher.
- His brother is a student.
- Her cat is small.

## Latihan A — Complete with my/your/his/her
1. I have a book. This is ___ book.
2. Budi has a bag. This is ___ bag.
3. Siti has a pencil. This is ___ pencil.
4. You have a pen. This is ___ pen.

## Latihan B — Put the words in order
1. my / This / book / is
2. red / Her / is / bag
3. his / is / This / pen

## Kunci jawaban
A: 1 my, 2 his, 3 her, 4 your.  
B: 1 This is my book. 2 Her bag is red. 3 This is his pen.

## Alur 40 menit
- Retrieval nouns yang familiar: 5 menit
- Model dan gesture kepemilikan: 8 menit
- Latihan melengkapi: 10 menit
- Pair activity menggunakan benda sekitar: 12 menit
- Exit check: 5 menit

## Evidence
Teaching log mencatat my/your/his/her digunakan dengan berbagai noun seperti book, bag, pen, pencil, teacher, mother, brother, sister, class, dan cat. Jangan menganggap semua bentuk sudah dikuasai tanpa evidence.
