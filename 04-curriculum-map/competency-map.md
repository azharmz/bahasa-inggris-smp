# Grade VIII Competency Map

## Status

`DRAFT v0.1 — EVIDENCE-BACKED, NOT YET SEMESTER-SEQUENCED`

This map translates the official Phase D destination and the Grade VIII textbook into operational competency strands. It is **not** yet the Semester 1 teaching order.

## A. Phase-Level Destination

### LS — Listening & Speaking
Students progress toward understanding flow, main ideas, and details in familiar oral texts and expressing ideas/experiences orally using increasingly connected simple/compound sentences in context.

### RV — Reading & Viewing
Students progress toward understanding flow plus explicit/implicit information in written/multimodal texts on familiar topics and responding in context.

### WP — Writing & Presenting
Students progress toward communicating ideas/experiences through written/multimodal texts with increasingly appropriate text structure/language features, including expressing opinions and supporting an argument.

## B. Grade VIII Textbook Competency Clusters

### C0 — Classroom Participation / Learning Language
Purpose: enable students to participate in English lessons and access later tasks.

Observable capabilities include progressively being able to:

- follow frequent classroom instructions;
- ask for repetition/clarification;
- ask how to say/spell/pronounce a word;
- participate in pair/group task routines;
- understand basic teacher task language.

**Role:** enabling/foundation competency, not a chapter that must be completed linearly.

### C1 — Recount Personal Past Experience
Textbook anchor: Chapter 1 and revisited/expanded in Chapter 4.

Progression:

1. recognize familiar past-event vocabulary and time reference;
2. distinguish/use common past forms (`was/were`, regular and selected irregular verbs);
3. understand specific information in short spoken/written recounts;
4. ask/answer simple questions about a past event;
5. sequence events chronologically;
6. orally recount a short personal/familiar event;
7. write a short series of past events with support;
8. move toward a more structured recount (orientation → events → optional comment/evaluation).

### C2 — Narrative / Story Comprehension and Retelling
Textbook anchor: Chapter 2 and Chapter 5.

Progression:

1. identify characters, setting, actions, and feelings;
2. understand chronological event sequence;
3. use past forms to describe what characters did/experienced;
4. use adjectives for traits/feelings;
5. identify main events and story meaning/message with support;
6. retell a familiar story orally using a model/script;
7. rewrite/write a short modeled story;
8. enrich event description using devices such as adverbs of manner.

### C3 — Procedure / Instruction
Textbook anchor: Chapter 3; procedure is treated by the book as a text type previously introduced in Grade VII.

Progression:

1. follow short familiar instructions;
2. identify purpose/materials or relevant information in simple procedure content;
3. recognize sequencing/order of steps;
4. orally present a short familiar procedure with support;
5. write/represent instructions for a practical purpose (including poster format).

### C4 — Opinion and Response
Textbook anchor: Chapter 3 and Chapter 5.

Progression:

1. understand simple opinion expressions in context;
2. give a short personal opinion (`I think...`, `I believe...`, etc.);
3. ask for another person's opinion;
4. respond appropriately/agreement-disagreement at an accessible level;
5. connect an opinion to a simple reason/example;
6. progress toward the Phase D destination of expressing and supporting an argument.

**Boundary:** the textbook's simple opinion work is a bridge toward the broader Phase D CP; it should not be misrepresented as full argumentative mastery.

### C5 — Information Extraction Across Modes
Cross-cutting cluster supporting all textbook chapters and all CP elements.

Progression:

1. identify familiar words/phrases with contextual support;
2. locate explicitly stated specific information;
3. identify who/what/when/where and key actions;
4. identify main idea/topic;
5. reconstruct overall flow/sequence;
6. infer simple implicit meaning using context;
7. respond to information orally, in writing, or multimodally.

### C6 — Supported Production → Independent Production
Cross-cutting Genre-Based Approach progression.

Progression:

1. build topic/context vocabulary and knowledge;
2. notice language/text features from models;
3. reproduce/complete modeled language;
4. jointly/guidedly construct an output;
5. independently produce a short output appropriate to current competence;
6. reflect/revise based on evidence/feedback.

## C. Language Resources — Enablers, Not Standalone Curriculum

The following resources recur in the Grade VIII textbook and should be taught in service of the competency clusters above:

- subject/reference words and basic sentence relationships;
- `be` in present/past where needed;
- common present/past verb forms;
- regular and high-utility irregular past verbs;
- `did` + base verb for past questions/negatives;
- `was/were` questions;
- Wh-question words and question formation;
- adjectives for condition, feeling, and traits;
- time expressions and sequencing/connectives;
- opinion expressions;
- adverbs of manner;
- vocabulary tied to familiar school/home/community topics.

This list is **not yet a teaching sequence**. The prerequisite map determines dependencies; diagnostic evidence determines the actual entry point.
